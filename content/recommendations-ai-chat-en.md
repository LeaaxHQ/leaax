1. Go straight to the affected provider's share-link management and delete/remove the public link.
2. Check whether the chat contained sensitive information (passwords, customer data) — if so, change it immediately or inform anyone affected.
3. Going forward, think about whether "public" sharing is really needed before sharing an AI chat.

<details>
<summary>Provider-specific instructions</summary>

- **ChatGPT:** Settings → Data controls → "Shared links" → delete the affected link individually.
- **Claude:** Settings → Privacy → Data controls → "Manage" under "Shared chats" → "Unshare" the affected chat.
- **Gemini:** The link is tied to your Gemini Apps Activity — delete the corresponding chat from your Activity, which also removes the public link.
- **Grok:** Settings → Data Controls → manage/delete shared content there.
- **DeepSeek:** Settings → Data → Shared Links → Manage → delete the affected link.

These menu paths can change — when in doubt, the provider's own help page is the most up-to-date source.
